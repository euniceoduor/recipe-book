About LemonDrizzle

Inspiration...

It is simply about the food. I imagine love tastes like food; sweet and savoury, sugar and salt and spice. I love cakes, buns and tasty stews and trying out new recipes.

For almost a year, I have been writing my recipes at the back of my drawing book. I welcomed my baby girl recently and while home for maternity leave, I got cooking .. lots of cooking. Trying to keep track of how much sugar, milk, flour was just right for my cakes and bread has been hectic since I filled my drawing-book. I thought, why not make it a virtual book that I can edit, add photos and share with friends who are tantalized by my whatsapp posts of food.

My hope...

Is that this web-app is a space for your home-made recipes, your nana's recipes and recipes you want to try out. You will also find a community of friends who love to cook, bake and eat. You know, because a little cake is good for the soul.
